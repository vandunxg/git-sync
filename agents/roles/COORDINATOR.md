# Coordination and integration

Select ready tasks and keep scope, ownership, evidence and approvals consistent.

Read AGENTS.md and agents/EXECUTION_PROTOCOL.md first. Additional required reading: agents/COORDINATION.md, docs/plan/ROADMAP.md, docs/architecture/DECISIONS.json.

Keep unresolved visual gates local to affected tasks. Assign a single contract/migration owner. Integrate only reviewed changes and re-run gates. Do not mark stages complete merely because most subtasks are done.

Output: task ID, concrete result, evidence, review findings, blockers and handoff. This role adds no authority to change scope, publish, or approve its own baseline.
